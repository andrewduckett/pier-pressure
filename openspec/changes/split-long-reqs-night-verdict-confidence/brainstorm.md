| D6 | How does "The document carries cloud agreement" split? | Into two: the `agreement` object is always present, with a `cloud_cover` entry exactly when two providers share a scored hour; and that entry holds `band`, `spread` and `providers`. | Assumption. | D7 | How does "Cloud agreement is the clarity-weighted mean gap over the scored window" split? | Into three: an hour's gap is the highest reading minus the lowest, counted only where two providers cover it; the night's mean gap weights each hour by coverage times the clarity weight of its lowest reading, and is 0 when every weight is zero; the band is decided on the rounded spread. | Assumption. | D8 | How does "Confidence falls with low agreement" split? | Into four: confidence multiplies by an agreement factor set by the band, a tuning constant fixed in code; agreement alone never takes a `MEDIUM` or `HIGH` confidence below `MEDIUM`; agreement changes no gate, score or verdict; an alternate's issue time never changes confidence. | Assumption. | D9 | How does "Reasons name the agreement" split? | Into two: a `cloud_cover` agreement entry adds exactly one reason line, with its wording and suffix, and none appears without one; that line comes after the gate and score lines.
