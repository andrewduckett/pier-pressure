## Story

#240 split-long-reqs-ha-delivery-broker — the broker-connection requirements in ha-delivery each state one behavior.
https://github.com/andrewduckett/pier-pressure/issues/240

## Decisions

| # | Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Which requirements does this story split? | The 6 `ha-delivery` requirements the issue names: "The process restores delivery after a reconnect" (14 scenarios), "The process waits for the broker at startup" (9), "Availability reflects process liveness via last-will" (6), "Delivery failures are reported, not swallowed" (4), "An error about the broker settings shows none of their text" (6) and "Persistent operation with periodic republish" (3). The other long `ha-delivery` requirements are left for #241. | Evidence: #240 body; `openspec validate --specs --json` |
| D2 | Does a change already in flight touch `ha-delivery`? | No. Open pull requests #246, #255 and the stack's other layers do not edit `openspec/specs/ha-delivery/`. | Evidence: `gh pr list`; `gh pr diff <n> --name-only` |
| D3 | How does "Persistent operation with periodic republish" split? | Into two: the process runs persistently, publishes at startup and on an interval, and does not depend on Home Assistant; the four kinds of message are published retained. | Assumption (the user delegated the brainstorm). Evidence: `openspec/specs/ha-delivery/spec.md` |
| D4 | How does "Availability reflects process liveness via last-will" split? | Into five: the retained offline last-will; the retained online availability while running; a planned stop publishes a retained offline; the stop waits at most two seconds; a stop while disconnected publishes nothing. | Assumption. Evidence: `openspec/specs/ha-delivery/spec.md` |
| D5 | How does "Delivery failures are reported, not swallowed" split? | Into five: a failure is logged, not reported as success; a failed publish does not stop the process; a publish held while disconnected is not logged; a refused publish is logged once as a warning; a refused online availability is sent again with the next verdict. | Assumption. Evidence: `openspec/specs/ha-delivery/spec.md` |
| D6 | How does "The process waits for the broker at startup" split? | Into seven: keep trying at startup; after a temporary failure, pause and try again (with the three ways an attempt fails); pauses from 1 to 120 seconds; the immediate tries with one detail changed; each failed attempt is logged; nothing is published until the broker accepts; a rejected login at startup stops the process. | Assumption. Evidence: `openspec/specs/ha-delivery/spec.md` |
| D7 | How does "The process restores delivery after a reconnect" split? | Into ten: keep trying to reconnect; subscribe again and republish the last state; online availability after the republished state; the MQTT client may send a message again after a reconnect; a refused subscription is logged; a reconnect triggers no verdict; a reconnect keeps the startup order; the outage is logged; a rejected login during a reconnect keeps trying; the process holds its publishes while disconnected. | Assumption. Evidence: `openspec/specs/ha-delivery/spec.md` |
| D8 | How does "An error about the broker settings shows none of their text" split? | Into four: no value from the `mqtt` block; no name of an undefined key; the error still says what is wrong; the password is masked once the settings are valid. | Assumption. Evidence: `openspec/specs/ha-delivery/spec.md` |
| D9 | How many scenarios does this story add? | One: "A publish held during an outage logs no warning", under "A publish held while disconnected is not logged", the only new requirement with no scenario of its own. It restates the SHALL NOT its requirement keeps. `test_a_publish_paho_queued_while_disconnected_logs_no_warning` in `tests/test_publish.py` asserts it. The pull request lists it. | Assumption. Evidence: `tests/test_publish.py`; #230 Shared decision 4 |
| D10 | Is any quoted pointer, in any spec, aimed at a heading this story removes? | No. No spec quotes any of the six headings. Two unquoted pointers inside them, "as the reconnect requirement describes" and "as the startup requirement describes", become quoted pointers to the new requirements they mean. | Evidence: `grep` of `openspec/specs` |
| D11 | What about code and tests that name a removed heading? | Left as they are, because the epic changes no code or test. Three test docstrings restate a removed heading: `tests/test_broker_wait.py:1`, `tests/test_reconnect.py:1` and `tests/test_service_reconnect.py:1`. No code comment quotes one. The pull request lists all three. | Assumption. Evidence: #230 "Won't"; `grep -i` of `pierpressure` and `tests` |
| D12 | Does the code disagree with any of the six requirements? | No drift found in spot checks: the 1-second first pause and 120-second limit, the 2-second offline wait, the retained QoS 1 last-will, one warning per run of refusals, the re-sent online availability and the masked password match `pierpressure/delivery/mqtt.py`, `pierpressure/core/config.py` and `tests/test_publish.py`. | Evidence: those files |
| D13 | May the SHALL text be reworded? | Only so that each new requirement reads on its own, such as naming "at startup" or "the `mqtt` block" again. "The process does not hold such a message back" becomes "SHALL NOT hold such a message back", because its requirement needs a SHALL and its scenario already asserts it ("it does not withhold or remove the message"). That is +1 SHALL NOT; every other SHALL, SHALL NOT and MAY keeps its meaning and count. | Evidence: #230 Shared decisions 3 and 4 |
| D14 | Which words do the new headings use? | The spec's own words (broker, attempt, temporary failure, rejected login, immediate try, reconnect, last-will, planned stop, `mqtt` block). `openspec/glossary.md` defines no broker term, and no term is new. No ADDED heading repeats a heading the delta removes or one the spec keeps. | Evidence: `openspec/glossary.md`; #230 Shared decision 5 |
| D15 | What does "done" mean for this story? | `openspec validate split-long-reqs-ha-delivery-broker --strict` passes before archive; after archive none of the six named requirements, nor any of the 33 new ones, gives a long-requirement warning; the epic's check with `TARGET=split-long-reqs-ha-addon-broker` and `CAPS=ha-delivery` prints `OK: ha-delivery` with the 1 added scenario; `mise run check` is green. | Evidence: #230 Shared decisions 6 and 7; the stacked base, PR #260 |
