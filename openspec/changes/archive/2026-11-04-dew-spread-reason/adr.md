## Review Summary

ADR review completed for this change. Records 0001 to 0030 were read. The

## In-Force ADRs Reviewed

- [0001 Core is HA-agnostic and emits one JSON verdict contract](../../../docs/decisions/0001-core-ha-agnostic-json-verdict-contract.md): the document grows by one reason string only.
- [0002 Gates, then a banded score](../../../docs/decisions/0002-gates-then-banded-score.md): the dew line is neither a gate nor a score term.
- [0022 The core is the only place that validates settings](../../../docs/decisions/0022-core-is-the-only-place-that-validates-settings.md): `dew_margin` is validated by `PierConfig`, not the add-on.
- [0023 The verdict judges the remaining night](../../../docs/decisions/0023-verdict-judges-the-remaining-night.md): the dew walk reads the scored window.
- [0029 Forecast agreement is a confidence input](../../../docs/decisions/0029-forecast-agreement-is-a-confidence-input.md): the dew line follows the agreement line and leaves confidence alone.
- [0030 Raw input variables stay out of the verdict document](../../../docs/decisions/0030-raw-input-variables-stay-out-of-the-verdict-document.md): the spread is derived from two timeline variables and adds no variable of its own.

## New Durable ADRs Created

- None - no major durable architectural decisions were introduced. The

  from the code and the spec, and "a reason line only" is a requirement the
